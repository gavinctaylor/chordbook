Lyrics document format

Put each artist and song together on one line, formatted as Heading 1: Wheatus — Teenage Dirtbag. Put the lyrics underneath as normal text. Repeat for each song in the same document. Hyphen (-), en dash (–), em dash (—), a spaced | or colon also work between artist and song. Names must match your Sheet; capitalisation and extra spaces do not matter.
