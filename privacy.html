Your library stays on your device

Chordbook reads the Google spreadsheet and optional Google lyrics document you select, or CSV and Word (.docx) files you import. File imports are processed on your device without sending their contents to Google. Songs, chords, lyrics, the selected document and sheet references, import date and text-size preference are stored in your browser. The app does not send your imported library to its hosting server or to other users.

Google sign-in and the Drive chooser communicate with Google. Chordbook requests access only to files you select through Google’s chooser. The permission includes editing rights, but this app only reads your selected sheet and lyrics document. Google access tokens are held in memory, not saved with your songs.

The app uses no analytics or advertising scripts. The website host and Google may process ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, under their own policies.

To remove the saved library and preferences, clear this website’s browser data. You can also revoke Chordbook’s Google access in your Google account’s connections settings. Clearing data may remove offline access; keep your sheet, lyrics document or CSV as a backup.

Libraries do not automatically transfer between browsers, devices or website addresses. People sharing the same browser profile may be able to view that profile’s saved library.
